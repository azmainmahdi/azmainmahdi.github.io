About

I’ve always cared about how things look and how they work. It started with digital art and design, then branding work for clients. Product work opened that up into bigger questions. Building products around accessibility and usability made me curious about how people interact with technology, and how much that shapes everyday life. That led me to HCI.

Now I study what happens when AI starts designing the interfaces people use every day. More and more of what we tap, click and consume is drafted by an artificial intelligence. I want to understand how that changes the choices people make, and what kind of relationship people actually want with the machines around them.

Outside work, I like to travel, make digital art and build small games and web apps trying to make people's lives just a bit more convenient in my own way. Some hobbies
